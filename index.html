ABOUT JULIUS

More Than an Athlete.

I am Julius Williams III — an athlete, coach, author, and business professional committed to pursuing excellence while creating an impact that extends beyond competition.

Athletics are a major part of my story, but they are not the entirety of who I am. My journey is centered around performance, discipline, leadership, personal growth, creativity, and using the opportunities I have to positively influence others.

Whether I am competing, coaching, writing, developing professionally, or working with a brand, my goal is to represent myself with purpose and build relationships that are authentic, meaningful, and capable of creating long-term impact.
